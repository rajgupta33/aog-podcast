ABOUT THE HOST • SADHNA SHARMA

Meet Sadhna Sharma: Guiding Authentic Founder & Growth Stories

The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast is created and hosted by Sadhna Sharma in Dubai, UAE — bringing together entrepreneurs, brand builders, educators, and wellness leaders for candid long-form conversations.

Blending founder storytelling, personal branding, and practical mindset insight, each episode bridges the gap between building a successful business and sustaining personal fulfilment — without scripted answers or surface-level clichés.

Operated under UAE-licensed media and advertising entity Yellow Drapes LLC (Sharjah Media City Free Zone), the show serves both listeners seeking real-world perspective and founders looking to share their brand story.
