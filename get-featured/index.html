Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Booking a Guest Slot

Are guest appearances on Attitude of Gratitude paid?

Yes. Our commercial guest appearance packages start at AED 444 for a remote online recording session and AED 1,499 to AED 1,999 for in-person Dubai studio packages that include editing, multi-platform publishing, short-form reels, and paid social promotion. Every application is reviewed for topic and audience alignment before booking is confirmed.

What is the difference between the AED 444, AED 1,499, and AED 1,999 packages?

Starter Visibility (AED 444) is an online remote recording session only and excludes editing, platform publishing, and paid ads. Founder Authority (AED 1,499) includes an in-person Dubai studio recording, professional editing, publishing across our podcast/video platforms, multiple short-form reels, and at least 1 week of Meta/IG ad promotion. Market Leader Presence (AED 1,999) expands promotion to at least 1 month across Meta/IG and TikTok with an audience growth push.

Where does the studio recording take place?

Studio packages (Founder Authority and Market Leader Presence) are recorded in person in Dubai, UAE. Once your application is approved and your package is confirmed, our production team shares the studio coordination details, preparation notes, and available recording slots.

Who is a good fit to be featured on the podcast?

We feature startup founders, business owners, industry specialists, authors, educators, and wellness/mindset practitioners who have genuine experience and practical lessons to share with an ambitious audience.

How does the AED 250 Brand / Product Placement option work?

Brands can book a product or service placement (AED 250) to have their offering featured inside an episode. Paid placements and sponsorships are disclosed transparently in accordance with UAE media standards.
