Education Beyond Marks:
Parents, Students & Teachers Need This Conversation

What if success isn't about marks, but mindset?
Discover powerful lessons on parenting, student growth, confidence, and education from Sarika Govil.

22 May 202629 min 51 sec watch
YouTube Spotify Apple Amazon

About This Episode

In this episode of The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, host Sadhna sits down with Sarika Govil — an educator, mentor, and changemaker dedicated to transforming lives through education.

This isn't just a conversation about studies and classrooms.

It's about understanding children, building confidence, guiding students emotionally, and helping parents rethink success.

Sarika shares powerful real-life experiences from her journey, her work with students, and the importance of teaching with purpose rather than pressure.

What You'll Take Away

  • Why children fear studies and lose confidence
  • The biggest mistakes parents make
  • Science vs Arts — stop comparing children
  • Why emotional connection matters in learning
  • Student mindset and confidence building
  • Why marks do not define success
  • Teaching beyond books and exams
  • Supporting needy students
