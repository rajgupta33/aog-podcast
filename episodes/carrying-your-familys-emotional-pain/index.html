Are You Carrying Your Family’s Emotional Pain?

Dr. Tanu Kaushal discusses generational trauma, recurring emotional patterns, self-love and gratitude, sharing her perspectives on holistic and spiritual healing.

19 August 202655 min 4 sec watch
YouTube Spotify Apple Podcasts Amazon Music

About This Episode

What if some of the emotional patterns and fears you carry didn’t actually begin with you?

In this deep conversation on The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, holistic healer Dr. Tanu Kaushal joins host Sadhna Sharma to explore generational trauma, inherited emotional patterns, subconscious beliefs, energy healing, self-love, and gratitude.

Together they examine why certain emotional struggles and relationship dynamics appear to repeat across generations, why high-achieving individuals can still feel internally stuck even when life looks successful on paper, and how conscious self-awareness helps break long-standing emotional loops.

Dr. Tanu Kaushal also shares her perspective on the interplay between mind, body, emotions, and energy — discussing holistic modalities such as Reiki, aura sensitivity, past-life regression (PLR), Akashic meditation, and how daily gratitude shifts emotional resilience.

Editorial Disclaimer: This episode discusses personal experiences and holistic/spiritual perspectives for educational and conversational purposes. The views expressed by the guest represent her own perspectives and experiences. This content is not intended as medical or mental-health diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for licensed healthcare.

What You'll Take Away

  • How unexamined family emotional patterns and beliefs get passed across generations
  • Recognizing the signs of repeating emotional and relationship loops in your own life
  • Understanding holistic modalities including Reiki, aura awareness, and Past Life Regression
  • How to evaluate and choose an ethical, grounded holistic practitioner
  • The difference between guided hypnosis and Akashic meditation practices
  • Why emotional release, forgiveness, and daily gratitude are essential for inner peace
