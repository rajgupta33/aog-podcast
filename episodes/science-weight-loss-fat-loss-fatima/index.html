The Science Behind Weight Loss:
Stop Chasing the Scale, Start Losing Fat

Is losing weight really as simple as eating less and exercising more? Certified nutritionist and health coach Fatima breaks down the science of fat loss, metabolism, hormones, and sustainable transformation.

27 May 202656 min 17 sec watch
YouTube Spotify Apple Amazon

About This Episode

Real transformation begins when you understand how your body actually works.

In this eye-opening episode of The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, host Sadhna Sharma welcomes Fatima, a certified nutritionist, dietician, and health & fitness coach, for a science-backed conversation that challenges many of the biggest myths surrounding weight loss.

Rather than promoting crash diets or quick fixes, Fatima explains why sustainable fat loss is built on understanding your body's metabolism, hormones, nutrition, sleep, movement, and mindset. She simplifies complex health concepts—from calorie deficits and insulin response to cortisol, hunger hormones, and body composition—into practical advice anyone can apply.

Drawing from both her professional expertise and her own remarkable journey of losing 24 kilograms, Fatima shares how long-term transformation comes from building healthy habits instead of chasing temporary results. The discussion also explores why many people hit weight-loss plateaus, the importance of stress management and quality sleep, and how small lifestyle changes can produce lasting improvements in overall health.

Whether your goal is to lose fat, improve your energy, or simply understand your body better, this episode offers practical knowledge that empowers you to make informed, sustainable choices for lifelong wellness.

What You'll Take Away

  • The crucial difference between weight loss and fat loss
  • Why hydration, nutrition, and sleep matter as much as exercise
  • How calorie deficits actually work for sustainable fat loss
  • Practical portion control techniques using the Plate Method
  • Why stress hormones can make losing belly fat more difficult
  • How hunger hormones influence cravings and appetite
  • Ways to overcome common weight-loss plateaus naturally
  • The five pillars of long-term health and sustainable transformation

Featured Guest

Fatima

Certified Nutritionist | Dietician | Health & Fitness Coach

Fatima is a certified nutritionist, dietician, and health & fitness coach dedicated to helping individuals achieve sustainable, science-based health transformations. Her coaching philosophy focuses on long-term lifestyle changes rather than restrictive diets, empowering people to build healthier habits that improve both physical and mental wellbeing.

Having experienced her own remarkable transformation by losing 24 kilograms, Fatima combines professional expertise with personal experience to educate people on nutrition, metabolism, hormonal health, and sustainable fat loss. Through practical guidance and evidence-based strategies, she helps clients move beyond quick fixes and create lasting results.
