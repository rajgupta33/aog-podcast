More Than a Staycation:
Discover Fujairah's Ultimate Sports Retreat

What if your next getaway wasn't just about relaxing—but about reconnecting with family, staying active, and creating unforgettable memories? Join Hania, General Manager of Fujairah Tennis & Country Club, as she shares why this destination is unlike any other in the UAE.

About This Episode

A great vacation isn't just about where you stay—it's about how you feel when you leave.

In this refreshing episode of The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, host Sadhna Rishi travels to Fujairah Tennis & Country Club to explore a destination that's redefining the idea of a staycation. Joined by Hania, the club's General Manager, this conversation reveals how sports, community, and wellness come together to create an experience that goes far beyond a traditional beach holiday.

From internationally recognized ATP-approved tennis courts and family-friendly activities to wellness facilities, pet-friendly accommodations, and engaging summer camps, Hania offers an inside look at what makes the club one of the UAE's most unique leisure destinations.

The discussion also highlights the club's commitment to building a strong community, encouraging active lifestyles across all age groups, and creating an environment where families can disconnect from screens and reconnect with each other.

Whether you're planning your next family getaway, looking for an active weekend escape, or simply curious about one of Fujairah's hidden gems, this episode showcases why meaningful experiences are created through connection, movement, and positivity.

  • The benefits of choosing an active holiday over a conventional beach resort

  • The importance of community-focused hospitality
  • Why wellness and recreation can go hand in hand
  • The vision behind supporting youth sports and tennis development
  • How a positive guest experience begins with genuine care and service

Featured Guest

Hania

General Manager | Fujairah Tennis & Country Club

Hania serves as the General Manager of Fujairah Tennis & Country Club, one of the UAE's leading sports and leisure destinations. Passionate about creating memorable guest experiences, she oversees a facility that combines world-class sports infrastructure, family-friendly hospitality, wellness amenities, and community-focused programs.

Under her leadership, the club continues to promote active lifestyles, youth sports development, and meaningful experiences for guests of all ages. Her vision reflects a belief that great hospitality goes beyond accommodation—it's about helping people build lasting memories together.

"The best vacations aren't measured by how much you rest—they're remembered for the moments you create together."
