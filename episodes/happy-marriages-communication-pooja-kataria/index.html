The Truth About Happy Marriages:
It's Not Love—It's Communication

Why do so many relationships fail despite good intentions? Relationship counselor Pooja Kataria shares practical wisdom on communication, self-respect, emotional healing, and building stronger marriages that truly last.

🎯 Relationships & Mental Wellness 📅 Released 2026 ⏱ 62 min listen

About This Episode

Strong relationships aren't built by chance—they're built through understanding, communication, and conscious effort.

In this heartfelt episode of The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, host Sadhna Rishi welcomes Pooja Kataria, relationship counselor, energy healer, and women's mental wellness coach, for an honest conversation about what it truly takes to build healthy and lasting relationships.

After spending eight years in the banking industry, Pooja followed a deeper calling to help individuals and couples navigate emotional challenges, relationship conflicts, and personal healing. Drawing from both her professional experience and personal journey, she explains why many marriages struggle—not because of a lack of love, but because of unmet expectations, poor communication, and the gradual loss of emotional connection.

The conversation explores some of the most important yet overlooked aspects of modern relationships, including preparing for marriage, maintaining self-respect without damaging the relationship, resolving conflicts without involving others, and recognizing when professional support can make a meaningful difference. Pooja also speaks openly about difficult realities such as physical abuse, emotional neglect, and the importance of prioritizing personal safety and mental wellbeing.

Beyond marriage, this episode offers valuable lessons for parents, emphasizing how the way we raise today's children shapes tomorrow's relationships. From encouraging emotional expression in boys to modeling respect within the family, Pooja highlights how healthy relationships begin long before adulthood.

Whether you're preparing for marriage, navigating relationship challenges, or simply looking to improve the way you communicate with the people you love, this conversation offers thoughtful insights that can strengthen every relationship in your life.

What You'll Take Away

  • Why communication matters more than assumptions in every relationship
  • How unrealistic expectations silently damage marriages
  • The importance of maintaining self-respect without creating emotional distance
  • Healthy ways to resolve disagreements before involving family members
  • Why seeking counseling is a sign of strength, not failure
  • How emotional intelligence helps build stronger relationships
  • The role parents play in raising emotionally healthy sons and daughters
  • Simple daily habits that help couples stay emotionally connected

Featured Guest

Pooja Kataria

Relationship Counselor | Energy Healer | Women's Mental Wellness Coach

Pooja Kataria is a relationship counselor and energy healer dedicated to helping individuals and couples build healthier, more fulfilling relationships through emotional awareness, effective communication, and personal growth. After spending eight years in the banking industry, she chose to pursue her passion for supporting people through life's emotional and relationship challenges.

Her work focuses on empowering women to reconnect with themselves, strengthening marriages through better communication, and encouraging families to create emotionally healthy environments where every individual feels heard, respected, and valued.

"A happy marriage isn't built on perfect people—it's built by two people who choose to communicate, understand, and grow together every single day."
