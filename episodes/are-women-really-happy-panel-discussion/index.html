Are Women Really Happy— or Just Pleasing Everyone Else?

SheEO Summit × Attitude of Gratitude Panel Discussion in Dubai — five women leaders unpack people-pleasing, boundaries, self-worth, and emotional freedom.

15 November 202644 min 59 sec watch
Watch the full panel on YouTube

About This Panel Discussion

What happens when a woman finally starts choosing herself?

Behind every smile, there may be unspoken expectations, emotional struggles, self-doubt, and the constant pressure to put everyone else first. In this eye-opening panel discussion hosted by Sadhna Sharma, founder of SheEO Summit and host of The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, five inspiring women come together in Dubai to explore how happiness influences women’s psychological, mental, emotional, and physical well-being.

From childhood conditioning and limiting beliefs to people-pleasing, self-worth, emotional resilience, and the courage to set boundaries, this conversation goes far beyond the conventional definition of happiness.

One of the most thought-provoking moments challenges a deeply rooted belief: why do we teach daughters to stand up for themselves, yet sometimes forget to teach sons to respect women’s choices and boundaries?

Because true happiness isn’t about smiling every moment — it’s about understanding yourself, respecting your needs, and finding the emotional freedom to live life on your own terms.

Key Questions Explored

  • Are we truly happy, or are we seeking external validation?
  • Why do women often put their families' happiness before their own?
  • How do childhood experiences shape our confidence, emotions, and decisions?
  • Can setting healthy boundaries transform our closest relationships?
  • Why it is equally important to teach sons to respect women's boundaries
  • What true emotional freedom looks like in everyday life
