Dubai Real Estate 2026: Buy Now or Wait? The Truth About the Property Market

A conversation about buying property in Dubai, covering off-plan and ready homes, mortgages, escrow accounts, renting versus buying, and property-linked residency.

About This Episode

Thinking about buying property in Dubai in 2026? Is the Dubai real estate market heading for a crash, or is this still one of the biggest property investment opportunities for residents, NRIs and international investors?

In this podcast, Dubai property consultants Mr. Maithil Shah and Mr. Nabil join host Sadna to break down the reality of the Dubai real estate market and explain what every buyer should understand before investing.

We discuss Dubai property prices, off-plan vs ready properties, mortgages, down payments, non-resident property purchases, Dubai Golden Visa rules, investor visas, escrow accounts, choosing the right property broker, Dubai Creek Harbour, Dubai South, renting vs buying and much more.

Disclaimer: This podcast is for educational and informational purposes only. Property prices, lending criteria, visa requirements, laws, fees and regulations may change. Always verify current requirements with the relevant UAE authorities, banks and qualified legal or real estate professionals before making an investment decision.
