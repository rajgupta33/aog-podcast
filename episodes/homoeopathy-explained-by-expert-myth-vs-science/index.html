About This Episode

Is Homoeopathy a Myth… or Real Science?

In this episode of Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, host Sadhna Sharma sits down with Dr. Devinder Kapoor, a leading homeopathic physician specializing in gut health, mental wellness, and energetic medicine.

Homoeopathy is a 200-year-old system based on the principle of “Like Cures Like”. But beyond theory, this episode dives into how it works in real life.

Unlike modern medicine that focuses on symptoms, homoeopathy treats the person behind the disease — understanding emotions, stress perception, and individuality.

🔥 What You'll Learn

  • What is homoeopathy & how it works
  • Law of Similars explained simply
  • Why every patient is treated differently
  • Homeopathy vs Allopathy vs Ayurveda
  • Root cause healing vs symptom suppression
  • Why healing takes time (not quick fixes)
