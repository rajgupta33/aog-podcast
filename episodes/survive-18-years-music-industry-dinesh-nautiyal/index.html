What Does It Really Take to Survive
18 Years in the Music Industry?

Behind every song is a story of persistence, passion, and countless unseen struggles. Music composer and singer Dinesh Nautiyal shares his journey of building a career in music, embracing creativity, and staying true to his dreams.

About This Episode

Talent may open the first door—but perseverance keeps you in the room.

In this inspiring episode of The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, host Sadhna Rishi sits down with Dinesh Nautiyal, a music composer, singer, songwriter, and producer, to explore the realities of building a life in one of the world's most competitive creative industries.

With over 18 years of experience, Dinesh reflects on the journey that began with a simple compliment about his voice and evolved into a lifelong passion for creating music. From writing lyrics and composing melodies to recording, producing, and mastering tracks, he shares what truly goes into bringing a song to life.

The conversation goes beyond music itself. It dives into the uncertainty that artists face, the emotional highs and lows of pursuing a creative career, and why success often depends on resilience, continuous learning, and the courage to keep going even when results aren't immediate.

Whether you're an artist, entrepreneur, student, or anyone chasing a dream, this episode is a reminder that meaningful success is built through patience, consistent effort, and an unwavering belief in your purpose.

What You'll Take Away

  • What it really takes to produce a professional song

  • How to stay motivated through uncertainty and setbacks
  • Why learning never stops, regardless of experience
  • The importance of intention, discipline, and consistent effort
  • Practical lessons for anyone pursuing a creative passion

Featured Guest

Dinesh Nautiyal

Music Composer | Singer | Songwriter | Music Producer

Dinesh Nautiyal is a composer, singer, songwriter, and music producer whose journey in the music industry spans more than 18 years. His work combines songwriting, composition, vocal performance, recording, and production, allowing him to bring every stage of the creative process together under one vision.

Driven by a passion for continuous learning and artistic expression, Dinesh believes that creativity flourishes through collaboration, openness to new ideas, and consistent dedication. His story reflects the resilience required to pursue a career in music while staying committed to authentic self-expression.

"Dreams don't become reality through talent alone—they grow through patience, persistence, and the willingness to keep creating even when no one is watching."
