She Built a Brand Around ONE Word — SABR

Pooja Raval, founder of SABR in Dubai, shares how patience, resilience and her own career journey shaped a purpose-led clothing brand.

About This Episode

How do you turn patience and resilience into a purpose-led fashion brand in Dubai?

In this episode of The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, host Sadhna Sharma sits down with Pooja Raval, founder of SABR in Dubai, who built a purpose-led clothing brand around one powerful word: SABR — patience, resilience, and the inner strength to keep moving forward before visible results arrive.

Her journey to becoming a fashion entrepreneur in the UAE was anything but linear: studying engineering in India, completing an MBA in Marketing, gaining hands-on experience in retail and FMCG, moving to the UK to study strategic marketing and consumer behaviour, navigating career uncertainty and sponsorship pressure, and eventually relocating to Dubai to build something of her own.

Pooja didn't want to launch just another apparel label with a motivational slogan printed on a T-shirt. She wanted every piece of clothing to act as a physical anchor — especially for founders, creators, and professionals navigating uncertain chapters — reminding them that meaningful outcomes require both patience and disciplined action.

Together, Sadhna Sharma and Pooja Raval unpack the consumer psychology behind purpose-driven fashion, why instant-gratification culture erodes founder confidence, how self-acceptance sharpens business decision-making, and why patience without consistent execution isn't truly SABR.

What You'll Take Away

  • How Pooja Raval translated a single philosophical concept (SABR) into a differentiated Dubai apparel brand
  • Why cross-disciplinary experience in engineering, FMCG, and UK consumer behaviour creates stronger founders
  • The difference between passive waiting and active patience (SABR) when building a business
  • How to design physical products that build emotional resonance and community loyalty
  • Navigating international relocations, visa pressure, and career pivots without losing self-belief
  • Why comparison on social media distorts timelines for early-stage entrepreneurs

Featured Guest

Pooja Raval

Founder, SABR | Fashion Entrepreneur & Strategic Marketer in Dubai

Pooja Raval is the founder of SABR, a Dubai-based purpose-led clothing brand built around the philosophy of patience, resilience, and mindful perseverance. With an academic background spanning engineering, an MBA in Marketing, and postgraduate specialization in strategic marketing and consumer behaviour in the UK, she combines analytical brand strategy with authentic storytelling.

Through SABR, Pooja creates apparel designed to remind entrepreneurs, creatives, and everyday builders that lasting growth happens beneath the surface long before external recognition arrives.
