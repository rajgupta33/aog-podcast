About This Episode

Every successful business has one thing in common—it knows how to tell its story.

In this insightful episode of The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, host Sadhna Rishi sits down with Rashi Sejpal, founder of iCraft Communications, to explore what digital marketing really means beyond social media posts and advertising campaigns.

Drawing from more than a decade of experience in branding, design, and marketing strategy, Rashi shares her journey from starting as a graphic designer to building a full-service marketing agency that helps businesses strengthen their digital presence and connect with the right audience.

The conversation goes beyond marketing tactics. It explores the relationship between business owners and agencies, why many entrepreneurs underestimate the value of professional marketing, and how even small businesses can build a strong brand with the right strategy and consistent execution.

Whether you're launching a startup, running an established company, or simply trying to understand how modern marketing works, this episode offers practical advice on creating long-term growth rather than chasing short-term results.

What You'll Take Away

  • What digital marketing actually involves beyond social media
  • Why branding and marketing should work together
  • Common mistakes business owners make when hiring agencies
  • How startups can market effectively on a limited budget
  • Why consistency matters more than viral success
  • The importance of setting realistic expectations with marketing
  • How strong communication builds successful client-agency partnerships
  • Why respecting expertise leads to better business results

Featured Guest

Rashi Sejpal

Founder, iCraft Communications | Branding & Digital Marketing Strategist

Rashi Sejpal is the founder of iCraft Communications, a digital marketing agency dedicated to helping businesses build meaningful brands and achieve sustainable growth. Beginning her career as a graphic designer, she gradually expanded her expertise across branding, strategy, content, and digital marketing, giving her a well-rounded understanding of how businesses succeed in today's competitive landscape.

With experience working closely with both clients and agencies, Rashi believes that successful marketing is built on collaboration, transparency, and long-term strategy rather than quick fixes. Her mission is to make professional marketing accessible to businesses of every size while helping entrepreneurs understand the real value behind thoughtful brand building.

"Marketing isn't about spending more money—it's about creating more value for the people you're trying to serve."
