Why 2026 will be the biggest opportunity for e-commerce in Dubai

From zero to scaling globally —what it really takes to build a successful online business in the UAE today.

1 February 202639 min 58 sec watch
YouTube Spotify Apple Amazon

About This Episode

Is now the best time to start an e-commerce business in Dubai?

In this episode, we sit down with Raina Lalchand, founder of Emarkiz.com and Emarkiz Web Solutions, to break down exactly how to start and scale an e-commerce business in Dubai and the UAE — even if you’re a complete beginner.
With Dubai rapidly becoming a global hub for digital entrepreneurship, this conversation dives into the real opportunities, strategies, and mistakes most people don’t see.
This is not just theory — it’s a practical roadmap for anyone looking to enter the world of Amazon UAE, Shopify, and global selling.


She opens up about:


  • Why 2025–2026 is the biggest opportunity window

  • How beginners can enter the market with clarity

  • The exact systems behind scaling an online business

What You'll Take Away

  • How to start an e-commerce business in Dubai step-by-step
  • Amazon UAE vs Shopify — where should beginners start
  • The most common mistakes new sellers make
  • Why product presentation & A+ content drives conversions
  • Legal setup basics: licensing, compliance & listings
  • How AI is changing product marketing and growth

Featured Guest

Raina Lalchand

Founder, Emarkiz.com | E-commerce Consultant

Raina Lalchand is the founder of Emarkiz.com and Emarkiz Web Solutions, helping businesses launch, optimize, and scale their e-commerce presence in the UAE and globally.

With deep expertise in Amazon marketplaces, product positioning, and digital growth strategies, she has helped numerous brands navigate the complexities of online selling.

Her work focuses on simplifying e-commerce for beginners while building scalable systems for long-term success.
