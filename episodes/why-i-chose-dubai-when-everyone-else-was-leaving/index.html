Why I Chose Dubai
When Everyone Else Was Leaving

What makes someone believe so deeply in a city that they build a movement around it?
Entrepreneur Sunny Dhillon shares his journey from Canada to Dubai, the story behind #IStandWithDubai, and why gratitude, friendship, and courage shaped his life.

About This Episode

Sometimes, the biggest opportunities appear when everyone else is walking away.

In this inspiring episode of The Attitude of Gratitude Podcast, host Sadhna Rishi sits down with entrepreneur and business owner Sunny Dhillon, whose unwavering belief in Dubai during one of its most uncertain moments sparked a movement that resonated with thousands.

While many expatriates were preparing to leave, Sunny made a different choice. Together with a close friend, he launched the now-recognizable #IStandWithDubai campaign—a simple idea that quickly became a symbol of optimism, resilience, and gratitude for the city he proudly calls home.

Beyond entrepreneurship, this conversation explores the deeper lessons behind success: building genuine friendships, embracing creativity, finding confidence through content creation, and choosing purpose over public opinion. Sunny also reflects on how social media, acting, and storytelling helped him overcome difficult phases of life while inspiring others to do the same.

This episode is a reminder that success isn't just about business—it's about the people you surround yourself with, the courage to stay true to your beliefs, and the gratitude to appreciate every opportunity life offers.

Featured Guest

Sunny Dhillon

Entrepreneur | Real Estate & Yacht Business Owner | Founder of #IStandWithDubai

Sunny Dhillon is an entrepreneur who has built successful ventures in real estate and the luxury yacht industry while becoming a passionate advocate for the city of Dubai. Originally raised in Canada, he made Dubai his home after recognizing the opportunities, safety, and quality of life the city offers.

Widely recognized for launching the #IStandWithDubai movement, Sunny believes that gratitude, resilience, and meaningful relationships are the true foundations of success. Through his work and content, he continues to inspire people to embrace positivity, remain authentic, and live life without fear of judgment.

"Life becomes extraordinary when you stop worrying about what people think and start living for what truly matters."
