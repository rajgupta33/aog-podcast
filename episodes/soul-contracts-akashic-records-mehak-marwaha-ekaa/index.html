Soul Contracts, Akashic Records & Kids' Career Mistakes Explained

Mehak Marwaha, founder of EKAA, joins Sadhna Sharma to explore breaking repeating emotional loops, Tarot life coaching, and guiding children's futures without crushing their spirit.

About This Episode

Breaking emotional patterns, soul contracts & conscious career guidance

Have you ever wondered why you still feel an unexplained pull toward someone who hurt you — or why, even after trying every prayer and spiritual remedy, life still feels stuck in the exact same loop?

In this honest conversation on The Attitude of Gratitude, host Sadhna Sharma sits down with Mehak Marwaha, who shares her journey from a corporate career in Dubai to becoming a mother, a daughter-in-law in a joint family, and the founder of EKAA — a name meaning “Oneness.”

Through EKAA Radiance, EKAA Rise, and EKAA Rejoice, Mehak and Sadhna talk openly about the three things every family silently navigates today: healing deep emotional wounds, guiding children in the age of AI without crushing their spirit, and remembering to celebrate life’s milestones along the way.

“From 40 years you are doing the same thing… do you really expect someone to change it for you in 60 minutes? I will not predict your future. I will guide you — it is up to you whether you take that step.” — Mehak Marwaha

What You'll Take Away

  • Why some friendships only last a season and why repeating karmic relationships return until the lesson is learned
  • The real difference between Tarot prediction and Tarot life coaching — and why healing cannot be forced
  • Why today's parents feel more confused than teenagers about career counseling, AI careers, and studying abroad
  • Avoiding the mistake of pushing children into streams only for prestige instead of building a meaningful profile from Grade 8 or 9
  • Why taking the stress out of life's milestones and celebrating small wins keeps families grounded
